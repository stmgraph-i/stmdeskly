Deskly is free to use. It is not a marketing platform, a payment platform, or a social network. It is a place to put your information so people can find you.

Do not put anything on your Desk that you would not want a stranger to see. If you want to keep something private, do not add it to your Desk.

8. Privacy

Here is what we collect and why. Nothing sneaky.

What we store

  • Account information. Your email address (to log you in), a hashed version of your password (we never see the original), and your WhatsApp number (used for the contact button on your Desk).
  • What you put on your Desk. Your name, role, tagline, services, prices, photos, location, hours, and any other field you fill in.
  • Your bot's knowledge. Prices and facts you have taught your Desk bot.
  • Visits. When someone opens your public Desk, we record the visit — the time, and where they came from (if their browser shares that). We do not record their name, and we cannot identify them.
  • Unanswered questions. When someone asks your bot something it does not know, we store the text of the question so you can see it on your dashboard and answer it. We do not store who asked.

Who can see it

  • You — see everything on your dashboard.
  • Visitors — see only what you have made public on your Desk.
  • Us — we can access the database, but we do not look at your data unless you ask us to help you with something.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell your information and we do not share it with advertisers.

Where it lives

Data is stored securely with Supabase, a database provider. Emails (like password resets) are sent through Gmail. Your photos are stored in Supabase Storage. No third party receives your data beyond what is needed to run the service.

Deleting your account

Write to us and we will delete your Desk and the data behind it. This usually takes a day or two. Once deleted, it cannot be recovered.

Cookies and storage

Deskly uses your browser's local storage to keep you logged in and to remember small preferences (like the accounts shown on the login page). We do not use tracking cookies.
