How it works

A short guide.

1
Create your Desk

Pick what the Desk is for: yourself, your business, or an organisation. Fill in the basics. You get a link right away.

2
Add what matters

Services, products, location, hours, or anything else. You can add it now or come back any time.

3
Share the link

Put it in your bio, your status, or send it directly. Anyone who opens it sees your Desk.

4
Keep it up to date

Change your tagline, add a service, update your hours. Your Desk updates the moment you save.

5
Your bot

A small helper that answers common questions for visitors while you are busy. You will be able to switch it on from your account.
